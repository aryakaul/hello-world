## Overall Impression

The remediation did what it set out to do, and the measurements confirm it rather than merely asserting it: every one of 18 text-contrast checks now passes (lowest 5.49:1), all four interactive borders clear 3:1, a uniform 3px 7.59:1 focus ring covers all seven interactive selectors, `th[scope]` is 100%, `document.title` is unique per route, the status region is correctly placed outside `<main>` and announces sanely, and the console is silent across every route including bad ones.

Two things stand out against that. First, the remediation introduced four new defects of its own — a 320px overflow, a stale ARIA reference, an invalid listbox child, and a tap target it missed. Second, and more important, the pass fixed the interface around the phrase without ever designing the phrase moment itself. ## What's Working

1. **The search ranking is the best-engineered thing here.** `matchRank` plus the rank-5 demotion of language-matched countries plus `+0.5` interleaving means `port` → Portugal, Portuguese, Brazil-speaks-Portuguese in that order, and `in` → India first out of 24. Measured across every real country name tried, the type-three-letters-and-press-Enter path could not be made to land somewhere absurd.
2. **Provenance was solved editorially, not decoratively.** `allDrafts()` + `hideDraftBadge` recognizes that 119 identical chips communicate less than one plain sentence, keeps per-entry badges alive for the day a verified entry appears, and `statusBadge()` still refuses to render "verified" without a real handle behind a regex guard.
3. **RTL and script handling are done from the data.** Verified live on 18 Urdu cells; the same test runs on slang variants. Paired with the `unicode-range`-scoped Ethiopic face, a new language file renders correctly with zero code change — real infrastructure for the contributor story.
4. **The keyboard model is genuinely correct.** Arrows with wrap, Home, End, Escape, `aria-activedescendant` tracking on every key, and the dropdown verifiably staying open when focus enters the list. B explicitly cleared "rows unreachable by Tab" as a false positive: that is the ARIA 1.2 combobox pattern working as intended.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The moment the product exists for has no design
**What:** no enlarge, no full-screen phrase view, no TTS (`speechSynthesis` is never referenced), and `audioButton()` is dead code that has never rendered because `data/index.json` has no `audio` key. The native script — the one thing a stranger could read off the screen — is 1.05rem in a **107px-wide column** at 390px.
**Why it matters:** every other decision on the page is justified by "the moment is the constraint," and the moment itself is unserved. The user gets a reference table and improvises the human interaction.
**Fix:** make the phrase row tappable to a full-screen view — native script ~48px, respelling ~28px beneath, English small above, one dismiss control. No new data, no new dependency; it turns a glossary into an instrument.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape`

### [P0] Search results are silent to screen readers, and the combobox goes stale on zero results
**What:** `fillResults()` never calls `announce()`, so neither a result count nor "no matches" reaches `#route-status`. On zero results it never calls `setActive()`, so `aria-activedescendant` retains its previous value — verified: after typing `xyzzy`, `.search` still reported `aria-activedescendant="result-0"` pointing at a removed node while `aria-expanded="true"`. Both assessments found this independently. Additionally `.result-empty` is a `<p>` injected as a direct child of `role="listbox"`, which is not a permitted child role.
**Why it matters:** Sam types a country, hears nothing, and the AT is pointed at a node that no longer exists — at the single entry point to the product.
**Fix:** announce the count or the no-match sentence; `removeAttribute("aria-activedescendant")` on the empty branch; move both `.result-empty` messages out of the listbox into a sibling `role="status"`.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Below the first screen the user has no controls at all
**What:** `#/india` is 21,737px at 390px. `.lang-nav` and `.back-link` live only at the top; the sticky `h2` is purely a label. Verified: after the jump-nav reached Urdu at scrollY 18525, `.lang-nav` bounding bottom was < 0.
**Why it matters:** Casey, one-handed in Chennai, wants Tamil after reading Urdu. The answer is 18,000px of thumb-flicking or a reload.
**Fix:** put the switcher into the sticky header — current language plus a compact prev/next or "languages ▾" — and add a back-to-top past ~2 viewports. The sticky region already exists and is carrying a third of the payload it could.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Search has no alias, exonym, or diacritic layer
**What:** verified empty results for `deutschland`, `holland`, `españa`, `espana`, `burma`, `united`, `japn`. `usa` returns Hausa and Nigeria as its only two hits. Country records carry no `native_name` and no aliases, and `matchRank` does a bare `toLowerCase()` with no normalization.
**Why it matters:** the primary user is standing in the country. The name on the signs and the money is frequently the endonym, and right now the endonym is a dead end.
**Fix:** add `aliases[]` and `native_name` to each country in the index; fold diacritics with `normalize("NFD").replace(/\p{Diacritic}/gu,"")`; require ≥4 characters before the rank-3 substring branch so three-letter queries cannot surface a mid-word match as their top hit.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Regressions introduced by the remediation pass
**What:** four defects that did not exist before commit c272ba1.
- **Horizontal overflow at 320px** — `#/india` 326 > 320 (+6px), `#/ethiopia` 323 > 320 (+3px), chain ending at `TD.respelling`. The pre-remediation run measured clean at 320.
- **`h2` language links are 97×23px** — 7 of 7 fail the 44×44 target, at both 390 and 320, while every other interactive element was brought to 44.
- **Stale `aria-activedescendant`** and the **`<p>` inside `role="listbox"`** (folded into the P0 above).
- **"Mexico, 1 languages."** — `announce()` concatenates without pluralization, and this string exists only for screen reader users, so the one audience it serves is the only one who hears the bug.
**Fix:** all four are small and local.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] The globe never re-measures and breaks on rotation
**What:** `mountGlobe` captures `size` once and writes it to inline `style.width/height`; there is no `resize` or `orientationchange` listener. Narrowing the viewport after mount leaves the canvas computed at **358px × 480px** over a 960×960 square bitmap — a visible vertical egg. Because `featureAt()` feeds raw `offsetX/offsetY` into `projection.invert`, hit-testing is mis-registered in that state.
**Why it matters:** rotating the phone is the most ordinary thing a one-handed mobile user does, and the browse path silently breaks and looks broken. This one predates the remediation.
**Fix:** a debounced `ResizeObserver` on `.globe-slot` that re-runs size/`fitExtent`/`draw`, preserving current rotation and clamped scale.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Contribution still outnumbers content 119-to-17 inside the table
**What:** one "suggest a fix" per entry per language — 119 on `#/india`, contributing 119 of the page's 142 tab stops. The first pass cut 238 → 119 and moved verify to section level, which helped, but for a keyboard user contribution is still the majority of the page.
**Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md requires contribution to stay unobtrusive and never compete with the lookup path. At one interleaved GitHub link per phrase it is still the dominant interactive element of a reading surface.
**Fix:** drop to a single per-section affordance — `.section-actions` can absorb "report a problem in this table" — or make the row hover/long-press revealed rather than a permanent tab stop. Removes 119 tab stops and roughly 5,000px of height.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

## Minor Observations

- No `<meta name="description">` and no Open Graph — a link shared with a fellow traveler previews as nothing.
- Flag emoji are concatenated into `h1`/`h2`, so screen readers announce "flag of India India."

- 75KB Ethiopic font loads on home despite `unicode-range`; something in the 117-pill list is triggering the range match.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the thesis is "the moment is the constraint," why is the moment the only thing on this surface with no interface? A full-screen phrase view is roughly 40 lines.
2. 119 "suggest a fix" links are shown to a traveler holding a phone toward a stranger. What is the cost-benefit of pre-paying for contribution with the primary user's screen and 84% of their tab stops — on a private repo with no users yet?
3. Is honesty a per-pageview obligation or a per-user one? A dismissible once-per-session note is equally honest and stops taxing the fastest path twenty times a trip.
4. "Warmth is the point." Point to the warm sentence on this surface. Every string is instructional, administrative, or apologetic.
5. If offline is a product feature, why is there no service worker? Today "offline" means "we make no external requests," a much weaker claim than the one PRODUCT.md makes.
6. The globe costs ~161KB over four sequential round trips and 320px of reserved blank space on the primary surface, to serve browse for a user defined as already knowing where they are standing. Should it sit below the country list, behind a disclosure?
7. Should the 68-pill Languages directory be on the home surface at all, given the positioning is explicitly country-first?
